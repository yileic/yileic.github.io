About Nuevo

Nuevo is a collection of contemporary residences built by award-winning builder SummerHill Homes:
  • Low Santa Clara Utilities & Resort-Style Living: Enjoy lower utility rates alongside premium community amenities, including a vibrant playground, basketball court, dedicated dog parks, and picnic areas.
  • The Bookmobile Advantage: Experience ultimate convenience with the Santa Clara City Library's Bookmobile making weekly, scheduled stops right outside your door.
  • Prime Silicon Valley Connectivity: Located just minutes from Costco, Whole Foods, and the Lawrence Caltrain station. Enjoy an effortless commute with easy freeway access and close proximity to major tech hubs like NVIDIA, Apple, Google, and Meta.
