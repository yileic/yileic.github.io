About Nuevo

A modern community at the doorstep of Lawrence Station.

Nuevo is a collection of contemporary townhomes and rooftop-terrace residences built around the new Lawrence Station district in Santa Clara. Designed by SummerHill Homes, the community brings clean architectural lines, private outdoor space, and walkable access to transit, dining, and tech campuses across the South Bay.

The neighborhood is organized into three home types — E-States, larger detached-feel residences with the most square footage; E-Towns, three-story townhomes with attached garages; and Terraces, more compact homes with shared rooftop decks — each offering a different balance of space, privacy, and price.

With Caltrain's Lawrence Station a short walk away and Highway 101 and 237 both minutes by car, Nuevo sits at the crossroads of Santa Clara, Sunnyvale, and San Jose — making it a popular landing spot for commuters working anywhere in Silicon Valley.
