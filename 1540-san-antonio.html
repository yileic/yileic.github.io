Looking for the perfect blend of style, convenience, and location? Welcome home to this stunning single-level condo just steps from everything you love about Menlo Park.

Why you'll love it here

  • ✨ Spacious: Open living/dining area featuring a cozy fireplace, elegant crown molding, and oversized picture windows that flood the home with natural light.
  • 🍳 Kitchen: Outfitted with granite countertops, island seating, and a dedicated dining area.
  • 🛏️ Primary Suite: Generously sized with an en-suite bath and your own private patio for morning coffee.
  • 💡 Perks: Secure elevator access, in-unit laundry, 2 assigned carport spaces, EV charging capability, and a private storage room.

📍 Location, location, location

You're perfectly positioned near Encinal Elementary, Menlo College, Caltrain, Stanford, and Meta. Even better — you're just moments from downtown Menlo Park and the vibrant new Springline development, your new go-to spot for incredible dining, coffee, boutique shopping, and the Sunday farmers market.
